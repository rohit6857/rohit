# Manali – Sissu Family Road Trip Itinerary
### 14 – 19 October 2026 | Self-drive from Greater Noida West | Maruti WagonR (CNG, 2021)

**Travelers:** Self, Wife, Daughter (6 yrs), Son (1 yr) — 4 pax
**Duration:** 6 days / 5 nights
**Season notes:** Mid-October is post-monsoon shoulder season in Kullu-Lahaul — clear skies, thin crowds, lower hotel rates. Expect 8–18°C in Manali and 2–14°C in Sissu, with a chance of early snow already dusting the higher peaks around Sissu/Atal Tunnel this year. Pack heavy woolens, especially for the kids.

---

## 1. Route & Driving Plan

**Total round trip:** ~1,150–1,200 km (Greater Noida West ⇄ Manali ⇄ Sissu)
**Route:** Greater Noida West → Delhi (bypass) → Karnal → Ambala → Chandigarh (NH44) → Kiratpur Sahib → Bilaspur → Sundernagar → Mandi → Kullu → Manali (NH21/NH3) → Solang Valley → Atal Tunnel → Sissu

With a 1-year-old and a 6-year-old, avoid one long 12–14 hr driving day in either direction. The plan below splits each leg into two comfortable days with an overnight halt around Chandigarh/Zirakpur (~260 km, the halfway point).

### ⚠️ Important — CNG car planning
Your WagonR CNG is a factory dual-fuel car (CNG + petrol tank), which works in your favor here:
- **CNG stations are plentiful only up to Chandigarh/Panchkula.** Fill the CNG tank to full before leaving Greater Noida West, and top up again at Chandigarh — that's your last reliable, easy CNG fill-up.
- **Beyond Bilaspur/Mandi/Kullu/Manali, CNG pumps are sparse and unreliable.** Switch to petrol mode from Chandigarh onward and keep the petrol tank topped up as your primary fuel for the hills (better throttle response on climbs too).
- On the return leg, switch back to CNG once you're back in Chandigarh/NCR range.
- Carry the vehicle RC, insurance, PUC, and driving license — there are police/highway checkposts, and occasionally a document check near the Atal Tunnel approach.

## 2. Day-wise Itinerary

### Day 1 — Wed, 14 Oct: Greater Noida West → Chandigarh/Zirakpur
- Start early (5:30–6:00 AM) to beat Delhi/Karnal traffic.
- Distance: ~260 km, ~5–6 hrs via NH44 with breaks.
- Fill CNG tank full at Chandigarh on arrival.
- Check into a budget hotel in Zirakpur/Panchkula for the night — easy highway access, good multi-cuisine food options for an early dinner.
- Early to bed — long driving day tomorrow.

### Day 2 — Thu, 15 Oct: Chandigarh → Manali
- Distance: ~310 km via Bilaspur – Sundernagar – Mandi – Pandoh – Kullu, ~7–8 hrs with stops.
- Breakfast on the way at Bilaspur/Swarghat (Gobind Sagar lake view dhabas are a nice stop with kids).
- Photo stop at Pandoh Dam / Beas river viewpoints near Kullu.
- Check into your Manali hotel by evening (see Hotels section). Relax, dinner at the hotel (MAP plan).
- Short evening stroll on Mall Road if the kids are up for it.

### Day 3 — Fri, 16 Oct: Manali local sightseeing (full day)
Easy, low-altitude sightseeing day — ideal with a toddler in tow.
- **Hadimba Devi Temple** (ancient cedar-wood temple in a deodar forest) — 30–40 min
- **Old Manali** — walk across the river, cafés, Manu Temple, laid-back river-side lanes
- **Vashisht Village** — natural hot water springs & old stone temple (great for a warm dip with kids, carry towels)
- **Club House Manali** — indoor games, roller skating, mini golf — good if weather turns cold for the little ones
- **Van Vihar National Park** — flat, stroller/pram-friendly walking trail along the river, deodar forest, boating
- **Tibetan Monastery (Himalayan Nyingmapa Buddhist Temple)** near Mall Road
- Evening: Mall Road for shawls, caps, dry fruits, and street food
- Overnight: Manali (2nd night)

### Day 4 — Sat, 17 Oct: Manali → Solang Valley → Atal Tunnel → Sissu
- Distance: ~50–55 km, ~2 hrs with photo stops.
- **Solang Valley** en route — paragliding/zorbing/ropeway if the kids' ages allow (mainly for adults; 6-year-old can enjoy the ropeway/cable car).
- Drive through the **Atal Tunnel** (9 km, world's longest highway tunnel above 10,000 ft) — a thrill by itself for a 6-year-old.
- Stop at the **South/North Portal viewpoints** for mountain panoramas.
- Check into your Sissu homestay by early afternoon.
- Afternoon: walk around **Sissu village**, **Sissu Waterfall**, and the banks of the **Chandra River** — flat, easy walking, stunning views, very few tourists.
- Dinner: home-cooked Himachali/Lahauli meal at the homestay (MAP).

### Day 5 — Sun, 18 Oct: Sissu → return halt (Chandigarh/Zirakpur)
- Morning: **Sissu Lake**, the suspension bridge over the Chandra river, and unhurried village walks — this is the scenic highlight of the trip, so keep the morning free and relaxed rather than rushing.
- (Optional, weather/road permitting, without the kids leaving the car for too long) short drive further toward **Gondhla / Khoksar** for a glimpse of the Lahaul valley, then turn back — do **not** push on to Keylong or Chandratal with toddlers; roads get rough and the altitude climbs fast.
- Start the return drive by early afternoon: Sissu → Manali → Kullu → Mandi → Bilaspur → Chandigarh/Zirakpur (~310 km, ~7–8 hrs).
- Overnight halt again at Chandigarh/Zirakpur.

### Day 6 — Mon, 19 Oct: Chandigarh → Greater Noida West
- Leave early to avoid the Monday morning Delhi-NCR office traffic on NH44.
- Distance: ~260 km, ~5–6 hrs.
- Reach home by early-mid afternoon/evening.

**Night-stay summary:** Chandigarh/Zirakpur (14th) → Manali (15th) → Manali (16th) → Sissu (17th) → Chandigarh/Zirakpur (18th) → Home (19th)

## 3. Where to Stay (Budget, ≤ ₹4,000/night, MAP – breakfast & dinner included, scenic view)

Prices are indicative for shoulder-season Oct 2026 and can shift — always confirm the **MAP (Modified American Plan)** meal inclusion and a **valley/mountain-facing room** directly with the property by phone/WhatsApp before booking, since OTA listings often show the cheapest non-view room by default.

### Manali (2 nights)
| Property | Area | Approx. Price (MAP) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vashisht Village property | Vashisht | ₹1,800–2,800 | Rooftop café with mountain panoramas, breakfast includes fresh parathas & local honey |
| Old Manali riverside guesthouse | Old Manali | ₹1,800–3,000 | Himachali wooden architecture, valley-view balconies, quieter than Mall Road |
| Alpine Comfort Inn | Between Old & New Manali | ~₹3,000–3,800 | Bay-window rooms for private mountain views, family rooms available |
| Shubdev Manali | New Manali | ₹3,000–4,000 | Clean, well-furnished, mountain/valley view rooms, good in-house food |
| The Orchid Hotel Manali | New Manali | ~₹3,000–4,000 | Free breakfast **and** dinner daily, mountain-facing rooms |

*Recommendation:* Call ahead and ask specifically for a family room with an extra bed/mattress for the kids and a valley-facing room — most of these properties can accommodate 4 pax in one room within budget.

### Sissu (1 night)
Homestays are the best value here — home-cooked meals (often breakfast, lunch *and* dinner) and unbeatable views of the Chandra valley and snow peaks, usually well under ₹4,000.
| Property | Notes |
|---|---|
| Milyang Homestay (near Gondhla/Sissu) | Breathtaking valley views, fresh home-cooked meals |
| Valley View Homestay, Gondhla | Garden + terrace, incredible mountain views, home-cooked local dishes |
| Snowflake Homestay, Sissu | Views of snow-clad peaks and green hills, home-made breakfast/lunch/dinner, flexible on budget |

*Tip:* Sissu homestays typically don't run heavy heating systems — request extra blankets/room heaters in advance given the 1-year-old, and confirm hot water availability.

### Transit halts — Chandigarh/Zirakpur (2 nights)
Any clean 2–3★ budget hotel or OYO/Treebo-listed property on the Zirakpur–Chandigarh NH44 stretch works fine here (₹2,000–3,000/night with breakfast) — this is purely a comfort halt to break the long drive, not a scenic stay.

## 4. Places & Sights to Visit

**Manali:** Hadimba Devi Temple · Old Manali · Manu Temple · Vashisht Hot Springs & Temple · Club House Manali · Van Vihar National Park · Tibetan Monastery · Manali Mall Road · Nehru Kund · Solang Valley (en route to Sissu) · Rahala Waterfall & Kothi/Gulaba viewpoints (on the Solang–Atal Tunnel road)

**Sissu / Lahaul:** Sissu Lake · Sissu Waterfall · Chandra River banks & suspension bridge · Sissu village walk · Atal Tunnel North & South Portal viewpoints · Gondhla (old fort/monastery, short drive) — keep Keylong/Chandratal/Baralacha for a future, kid-free, higher-altitude trip.

## 5. Food to Try

- **Himachali Dham** — traditional festive thali (rice, dal, madra, khatta, chana, sepu vadi) — try at a proper local dhaba, not a tourist restaurant
- **Siddu** — steamed wheat bread stuffed with poppy seed/walnut paste, served with ghee & chutney — a Manali/Kullu specialty, kid-friendly
- **Trout fish fry/curry** — fresh Kullu-Manali trout, widely available in Old Manali and Manali cafés
- **Chana Madra** — chickpeas in a yogurt-based curry
- **Babru** — Himachali version of kachori, served with tamarind chutney
- **Aktori** — sweet buckwheat pancake, a Lahauli/Kullu tea-time treat
- **Thukpa & Momos** — Tibetan influence is strong in Old Manali cafés, great warm comfort food for kids too
- **Sissu/Lahauli home-cooked meals** — if staying at a homestay, ask for local Lahauli dal, seasonal vegetables and fresh rotis
- **Apple products** — fresh apples, apple juice, and apple crumble/pie at the many bakery-cafés (try German Bakery-style cafés in Old Manali)
- **Roadside chai & roasted corn (bhutta)** — at Solang and Atal Tunnel viewpoints
- **Maggi points** — a Himalayan road-trip ritual at almost every viewpoint dhaba

## 7. Other Suggestions

- **Kids & altitude:** Sissu sits at ~3,100 m. Mild breathlessness/fussiness in the 1-year-old is possible — keep them hydrated, dress in layers, and avoid rushing further up to Keylong/Chandratal.
- **Baby essentials:** Diapers, formula, and baby food are not reliably available in Sissu village — stock up in Manali before heading up.
- **Medical:** Carry a basic family medical kit (fever, cold, motion sickness for the 6-year-old, ORS, thermometer). Nearest well-equipped hospitals are in Kullu/Mandi; Sissu has only a basic health post.
- **Connectivity:** Jio/Airtel work in Manali but get patchy past Solang/Atal Tunnel and in Sissu. Download offline Google Maps for the Manali–Sissu–Lahaul stretch before you go.
- **Car seat/safety:** Carry a proper car seat for the 1-year-old for the long highway stretches; hill roads have sharp curves near Kullu-Manali and again on the Atal Tunnel approach.
- **Warm clothing:** Nights in Sissu can dip close to 0–2°C in mid-October — carry heavy jackets, monkey caps, gloves, and thermals for both kids.
- **Booking timing:** October is shoulder season with thinner crowds — you can often negotiate a better MAP rate by calling hotels/homestays directly instead of booking through an OTA, and specifically ask for a valley-facing room.
- **Avoid Rohtang Pass proper** — it needs a permit and isn't necessary since the Atal Tunnel already gives you the same high-altitude scenery en route to Sissu without the permit hassle or crowds.
- **Return traffic:** You return to NCR on a Monday (19th) — leave Chandigarh/Zirakpur early to avoid the Panipat–Delhi morning office-traffic crawl.
